Features

Built to be shared.

Make characters and charts, share them, and compete on every song.

Steam Workshop

Custom characters

Upload your own custom character to Steam Workshop, and anyone can download it and run with it.

Share

Share your beatmaps

Chart a song your way in the editor, then share the beatmap so other players can play it too.

Compete

Leaderboards and challenges

Challenge friends on any chart and see who tops the leaderboard.

Play it your way.

Plug in a PlayStation or Xbox controller, use the Steam Deck’s built-in controls, or stick to the keyboard. Most other gamepads work through Steam Input too.
