Play it your way.

Pick up a PlayStation, Xbox or Nintendo controller, use the Steam Deck’s built-in controls, or stick to the keyboard. Most other gamepads work through Steam Input too.

Modes

Play alone, or together.

Solo, local co-op and versus, all on the same songs. Co-op and versus work with any two controllers, or with both players on one keyboard.

1 player

Solo

Just you and the song, both lanes to yourself. Chase your best score, push for an S rank and climb the leaderboards.

2 players

Co-op

Two players, one lane each, clearing the song together. Grab any two controllers, mix and match them, or share one keyboard: one player on WASD, the other on the arrow keys.

2 players

Versus

Same song, head to head. Plug in any two controllers or share the keyboard, and whoever plays it better takes the win.

Jukebox

A music player, built in.

Your whole library, by song, artist or playlist. Listen, pick something to play, or just leave it running.

Mini player

Take it with you

Fold the jukebox down into a pocket-sized player that floats over your desktop, so the music keeps going while you do other things.
